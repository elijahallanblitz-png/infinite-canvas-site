# Exercise recognition catalog — Gabby Reese / "Leggo" circuits

Reference for visually identifying and labeling exercises from silent video
frames. Each entry: how it **looks** (start position → motion) and the **tell**
that separates it from look-alikes. Grouped by the three circuits in the source
PDF (likely three different athletes/segments): **Sonny**, **Billy**, **Heather**.

How to use when labeling: first read the body orientation (standing / hinged /
on the floor / inverted), then the equipment in hand (none / DB / KB / ball /
bench), then the motion. Those three cut the candidate list down fast. Items
flagged ⚠️ are ambiguous from the name alone — confirm against the footage.

## Cross-circuit disambiguation (the easy-to-confuse buckets)

- **Squat family** — depth and what's in the hands is the tell:
  *Body/prisoner squat* = no weight (prisoner: hands behind head). *Goblet/KB
  goblet* = one weight held at chest. *DB/down* squat = weights hanging at
  sides. *Front squat* = weight in front-rack at shoulders. *Pump/pulse squat* =
  stays at the bottom and bounces, never stands tall. *Squat jump* = leaves the
  floor. *Thruster* (squat-to-press) = stands and presses overhead.
- **Lunge family** — which way the stepping leg goes:
  *Front/forward* = step forward. *Reverse/back* = step back. *Side* = step
  laterally, one leg stays straight. *Curtsy / figure-4* = step diagonally
  *behind* and across. *Split squat (fixed)* = feet stay planted, just up/down.
- **Hinge vs squat** — RDL/straight-leg deadlift = hips travel *back*, torso
  folds toward parallel, legs near-straight, back flat. Squat = hips drop
  *down*, torso stays more upright.
- **Plank family** — *Plank* static; *up-down/commando* alternates high↔forearm;
  *shoulder taps* stay in high plank tapping shoulders; *side plank* on one arm;
  *bear* = on hands + toes with knees bent and hovering.
- **Row vs fly (bent over)** — *Row* pulls elbows back toward the hips. *Rear
  delt fly* sweeps arms out wide to the sides, near-straight.

## Sonny

- **Body squat** — bodyweight squat, nothing in hands, hips to ~parallel, stand tall.
- **Pump squat** — holds the bottom of a squat and pulses up/down a few inches; never fully stands between reps. Tell: small bounces, short range.
- **High knee run** — run in place, knees driven to hip height, upright torso, fast.
- **Mountain climbers** — high plank, alternating knees driven rapidly toward chest; body horizontal.
- **Bent over row** — hinged ~45°, weight(s) pulled to the ribs, elbows travel back past the torso.
- **Bicep curl** — standing, upper arms pinned to sides, forearms curl the weight up.
- **Push up to open up** — push-up, then rotate the whole body into a side plank, top arm reaching to the sky (a "T"). Tell: the rotation/reach-up after each push-up. (a.k.a. T push-up / push-up with rotation.)
- **Push up knee** ⚠️ — most likely a knees-down (modified) push-up; could be a push-up driving a knee toward the elbow. Confirm: are knees on the floor, or knees driving in?
- **Squat to bicep curl** — squat down; on the rise, curl the weights. Combo.
- **Bicep curl to shoulder press** — standing; curl the weight, then press it overhead. Two-part combo.
- **Curtsy lunges** — step one leg diagonally *behind and across* the other, both knees bend, torso stays square/forward. Tell: the cross-behind diagonal.
- **Squat** — generic squat (read hands for load).
- **Alternating side lunge with KB** — step out laterally, that knee bends while the other leg stays straight, holding a kettlebell (usually at chest); alternate sides.
- **Back lunge to front kick** — reverse lunge, then drive the same leg forward into a front kick. Tell: lunge back → kick front, same leg.
- **Kettlebell goblet squat** — squat holding a KB at the chest with both hands.
- **Squat jumps** — squat then explode off the floor, land back into a squat. Airborne.
- **Lower leg lifts, hands under glutes** — lying on back, hands tucked under the glutes, legs raise and lower. Supine.
- **Plank** — static forearm or high plank hold.
- **Plank with knee to elbow** — from plank, drive a knee to the same-side elbow, alternating.
- **One leg RDL row** — balance on one leg, hinge forward (other leg extends back to parallel), and row a weight at the bottom. Tell: single-leg hinge + pull.
- **Down dog to opposite ankle** — downward-dog (hips high, inverted V), reach one hand across to the opposite ankle, twisting; alternate.
- **High knee run** — (repeat) run in place, high knees.
- **Jumping jacks** — arms and legs out/in with the jump.
- **High knees** — run in place, knees high (same look as high-knee run).
- **Spider man with a Y** ⚠️ — plank/push-up bringing a knee to the outside of the same-side elbow (spiderman), paired with reaching the arms overhead into a "Y." Confirm the Y-reach against footage.
- **Alt front lunge** — alternating forward lunges, bodyweight.
- **Alt kettlebell front lunge** — alternating forward lunges holding a KB.
- **Alt reverse to forward lunge** — same leg does a reverse lunge then a forward lunge; alternate sides.
- **Goblet squat** — squat holding one weight at the chest (DB or KB).
- **Kettlebell goblet squat** — (repeat) goblet squat, KB.
- **Straight leg dead lift** — hip hinge, legs near-straight, weight lowers down the legs, flat back. Hinge not squat.
- **Up and down fixed split squat** — feet in a stationary split stance (front foot flat, back foot planted), lower and rise in place. Tell: feet never move.
- **Glute bridges** — lying on back, knees bent, drive hips up. Supine.
- **Downbell squat** ⚠️ — read as a squat holding dumbbell(s) hanging at the sides / a single DB. Confirm load against footage.
- **Bear stance hold** — on hands and toes, knees bent and hovering ~1–2 in off the floor, flat back; static.
- **Bear row** — bear stance (knees hovering), row one DB to the hip, stay stable.

## Billy

- **Burpee** — from standing: drop to plank (often a push-up), jump feet in, jump up. Full floor-to-stand cycle.
- **Plank mountain climbers** — mountain climbers held in plank (knees drive to chest).
- **Dips with bench** — hands on a bench behind, feet out front, bend/extend elbows. Triceps.
- **Fly** — chest fly: arms open wide and close with a soft elbow bend (lying on bench or standing).
- **Insanity push ups** ⚠️ — explosive/wide push-ups (hands may leave the floor) done fast. Tell: power/plyo or wide hand stance.
- **Kbell handle shoulder press** — overhead press holding a kettlebell by its handle (bell up/over the wrist).
- **Plank with alt kettlebell slide** — in plank, push/slide a KB from one hand to the other across the floor; alternate.
- **Pushup w/ alt shoulder tap** — push-up, then in high plank tap the opposite shoulder; alternate.
- **Pushups** — standard push-up.
- **Diamond push ups** — hands together under the chest forming a diamond. Triceps; narrow hands.
- **DB front raises** — raise DBs straight out in front to shoulder height.
- **Rear delt fly** — bent over, sweep DBs out wide to the sides, near-straight arms. (Wide arc, not elbows-back.)
- **Rear delt fly bicep curl** — combo: a rear-delt fly and a curl.
- **Tricep kickbacks** — bent over, upper arm pinned, extend the forearm straight back.
- **Up up down down plank** — plank up-downs / commando: high plank → both forearms down → both hands back up; alternate the leading arm.
- **Hammer curls** — curls with a neutral grip (palms facing each other).
- **DB 21s** — curls in three blocks: 7 partial reps bottom-to-mid, 7 partial mid-to-top, 7 full. Tell: short low pulses, then short high pulses, then full curls.
- **DB bicep curl to shoulder press** — curl, then press overhead. Combo.
- **Bench back row** — bent-over/bench-supported row, pulling weight to the hip.
- **Alt step back lunge with a swing** — reverse lunge alternating with an arm/KB swing for momentum.
- **Butt kicks (light run)** — jog in place kicking heels up toward the glutes.
- **Fixed split squat, arms up, with weights / other side** — stationary split squat holding weights overhead; each side.
- **Front squat with a press** — front-rack squat, then press overhead (thruster).
- **KB front squat** — squat with KB(s) racked at the shoulders.
- **Prisoner squat** — bodyweight squat with hands clasped behind the head.
- **Squat DB** — squat holding dumbbells (at sides or shoulders).
- **Body squat** — bodyweight squat.
- **Squat to standing shoulder press** — squat, then overhead press (thruster).
- **Squat to alt shoulder press (standing)** — squat, then a single-arm overhead press; alternate arms.
- **Squat to step back lunge alt** — squat, then a reverse lunge; alternate sides.
- **Elbow plank with hip drop / up and down / other side** — forearm/side plank dropping the hip toward the floor and back; each side. (Side-plank hip dips.)
- **Side plank with reach under** — side plank, thread the top arm under the torso (rotate), then reopen.
- **Side plank hold** — static side plank on one forearm.
- **Stability ball (feet on ball push)** — feet on a stability ball in plank/pike; push or pike the ball (core/hamstrings).
- **Stability ball chest press** — upper back on the ball, hips up, DB chest press.
- **Russian twist with medicine ball (feet on ground / advanced)** — seated, leaning back, rotate a med ball side to side; feet down (basic) or lifted (advanced).
- **Plank lateral walks** — in plank, step hands+feet sideways (two over, two back).
- **KB squat to halo alternating** — goblet/KB squat, then circle the KB around the head (halo); alternate direction.

## Heather

- **Bounding** — exaggerated leaping run, long airborne strides.
- **Broad jump** — two-foot standing jump forward, land on both feet.
- **Skater** — lateral bound side to side, landing on one leg with the other sweeping behind (speed-skater).
- **Step back runners pop up, left and right** — reverse lunge then explode up driving the rear knee to a hop/pop; each side. (Reverse lunge to knee drive, explosive.)
- **BD front raise** — (DB) front raise to shoulder height.
- **Real delt pump** — rear-delt raise held/pulsed (bent-over wide arms, small pulses). ("Rear delt pump.")
- **Upright row** — pull the weight straight up along the body toward the chin, elbows lead high.
- **1 leg RDL both sides** — single-leg Romanian deadlift, balancing, hinge with the free leg extending back; each side.
- **1 leg RDL with a row** — single-leg RDL plus a row at the bottom.
- **Floor bridges** — glute bridge on the floor (hips up).
- **1 leg pointed toe bridge (both sides)** — single-leg glute bridge, the lifted leg's toe pointed; each side.
- **1 leg back-through lunge with arms up (both sides)** — reverse lunge with the arms raised overhead; each side.
- **Split squat bicep curl (both sides)** — stationary split squat while curling; each side.
- **Straight leg dead lift** — RDL/SLDL hinge (see Sonny).
- **Bear alt row** — bear stance (knees hovering), alternating rows each arm.
- **Figure 4 lunge with a step back (both sides)** — step diagonally back and across (curtsy/cross-behind, legs form a "4"); each side.
- **Squat jump and spin squat** — a squat jump, then a jump with a 180° spin, landing in a squat. Tell: the in-air rotation.
- **Alternating diagonal lunges with weight (front only)** — lunge forward on a diagonal angle, holding weight; alternate, stepping forward only.
- **Spider man** — spiderman: from plank/push-up drive a knee to the same-side elbow (or a spiderman lunge); alternate.

## Notes for labeling

- Many moves repeat across circuits (body squat, squat, goblet squat, straight-leg
  deadlift, spider man, bear row). Use the circuit context + surrounding moves to
  pick the intended label.
- ⚠️ items (`Push up knee`, `Spider man with a Y`, `Downbell squat`, `Insanity
  push ups`) are best confirmed by watching one rep before committing the label.
- Combos (squat-to-curl, curl-to-press, squat-to-lunge) span ~2 seconds of two
  distinct positions — don't split them into separate clips; label the whole combo.
